# Enhance (M4 slice 8)

- **Built by Claude Code, to be tuned by Nevada's eye,** as the plan says. The design left no open questions: EDT-12 says what Enhance sets, and the technical design gives the inputs.
- **On the CPU, from the guide image,** not a GPU compute pass as the technical design first said. The guide image is the photo at 1024 pixels on its long side, which the Editor already keeps. Reading it takes milliseconds, and it gives the same answer whatever size the photo is decoded at, so slice 9's grid will match the Editor. The technical design is corrected to say so.
- **It reads the photo as cropped and turned, never its tone settings,** so pressing it again sets the same values. That changes nothing and adds no record, as EDT-12 asks.
- **White balance needed two tries.**
  - The first version averaged the pixels within a fixed distance of gray. A strong cast tints the grays past that distance, so a blue cast went uncorrected.
  - The second found grays relative to a rough cast from every midtone. But a large blue sky skewed that rough cast, and the true grays were rejected again.
  - The method that works finds near-neutral pixels in steps, loosely at first and then more strictly, each time measured against the cast found so far, each weighed by how near gray it is.
- **A known limit, written down as a test:** under a warm cast, a blue sky looks nearly gray. When the sky fills much of the photo it outweighs the true grays, so the warmth stays. Outdoors that's usually right, since late-afternoon light is meant to look warm. Indoors under tungsten there's no sky, and the warm cast is taken out. The correction takes 80% of a cast, at most 60 on the slider.
- **The numbers so far,** all to be tuned:
  - **Exposure:** moves the log-average 60% of the way to middle gray, keeping the 99.5th percentile within half a stop past white, from −2 to +2 stops.
  - **Contrast:** up to 40, when the 0.5th to 99.5th percentile spread is under 0.85 of the range.
  - **Shadows and highlights:** up to 60 each, from the share of the photo that's very dark (below 0.15) or very bright (above 0.9) after exposure.
  - **Saturation:** up to 20 for a dull photo, never down.
- **First test photos were wrong too:** the "well exposed" scene had a sky at 0.58 in linear light, far brighter than real photos average, so Enhance rightly darkened it. The scenes now sit near middle gray.
- **Nevada tried it on several photos:** "Subjectively, it does appear to do a decent job of making photos look generally better." Nevada accepted it as it is: an opinionated all-in-one, as long as it follows the generally accepted way of making these adjustments, which it does. Merged without further tuning (#182). This note came after the merge, so it went on slice 9's branch rather than restart #182's CI.
