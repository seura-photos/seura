# Revert to original, pinned (design session)

- **Nevada found,** trying effects (M4 slice 6), that the Editor's tool panel now scrolls, so Revert to original sat below the window and its confirmation opened further below, out of sight.
- **Claude Code proposed** pinning Revert to the panel's bottom and opening its confirmation upward, keeping Cancel under the pointer (INT-5). Claude agreed, and also pinned the first row, Compare and Done, at the top, since INT-5 puts them there to be in reach and a scrolled-away Done defeats that. The confirmation is a card with a shadow over the panel; a soft shadow at the scrolling area's edges shows there's more.
