# Try Again, and OneDrive's own Cancel

- **Nevada found** that while Seura downloads a cloud-only photo, Windows sometimes shows OneDrive's own notice in the corner of the screen, with a Cancel button. He cancelled one: Seura said the photo couldn't be downloaded and offered Try Again, as LIB-9 asks, and nothing crashed. So the user can cancel a download after all, through the sync service rather than Seura.
- **But Try Again did nothing:** OneDrive's notice flickered and came back, and only going back to the grid and opening the photo again worked.
- **The cause, in Claude Code's Viewer core:** it remembered a failed decode alongside the decoded photos, and Try Again, asking for the same photo, got the remembered failure straight back; closing the Viewer cleared it. A photo that failed is now tried again whenever it's asked for, with a test that fails without the fix.
