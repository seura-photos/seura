# 2026-10-18: Photos that live in the cloud

Nevada raised it: many people's phone photos reach their computer through a cloud service's sync folder, like his own OneDrive "SkyDrive camera roll" (named from when OneDrive was SkyDrive). Those folders hold cloud-only files, and an app that reads them downloads them. He asked whether Seura can tell, and avoid downloading until a photo is really needed, and for a matrix of the services phone users rely on: OneDrive, iCloud, Google, and Dropbox, by platform.

- **A misunderstanding cleared up first:** Seura doesn't exclude synced folders from the library. SET-1 is only about keeping Seura's own cache out of a synced Documents folder.
- **Slice 3 already handled scanning.** Claude checked it on Nevada's camera roll (3,947 files, 3,885 cloud-only, 21.4 GB): Seura's detection matched Windows' count exactly, and after a full scan every cloud-only file was still cloud-only. The two photos the scan skipped turned out to be in a `.picasaoriginals` folder, Picasa's backups of photos Nevada once edited there, correctly skipped as hidden.
- **The research** (`docs/research/cloud-photo-sources.md`) found per-file detection on both platforms for OneDrive, iCloud Drive, and the Mac clients of Google Drive and Dropbox; Google Drive on Windows needs treating as cloud-only throughout. Two findings go beyond downloading: on a Mac, iCloud Photos lives inside the Photos app's library rather than a folder, and Google Photos has no desktop sync at all, so those users' photos can't reach a folder-based app automatically. Also new since the design: Samsung Gallery's direct OneDrive sync ended on 2026-09-30.
- **Proposed by Claude, for Nevada's review:** LIB-9 (cloud-only photos are shown without downloading, and downloaded one at a time only when needed), a "Cloud-only photos" section in the technical design, and slice 4 gaining a cloud-only check, with Nevada's camera roll in its "done when".
