# Project journal

Working notes for the devlog on the [Seura Photos website](https://seuraphotos.app/devlog/). The devlog tells the story of how Seura is built; these notes are its raw material, written while the details are fresh.

Add a dated entry (`YYYY-MM-DD-short-topic.md`) whenever something worth telling happens:

- a decision, and why it went that way (link the decision record if there is one);
- something that didn't work, or turned out harder than expected;
- a mistake caught, by Nevada or by Claude, and how;
- a screenshot-worthy moment (save the image beside the note).

**Consider everything for the devlog** (Nevada, 2026-10-23): every slice, fix, finding, decision, and change of course gets at least a short entry, however small it seems. Deciding what goes into a post happens when the post is drafted, not when the entry is written, and the draft should account for what it leaves out.

Both sides add entries: Claude Code while implementing, and Nevada's design sessions with Claude in the Claude app when a design decision is made. Ideas from outside the project, such as a friend's suggestion, belong here too. Keep entries short: a few bullet points is fine. Say who suggested or decided what, and which session it came from. At the end of each milestone, a devlog post is drafted from these notes in the website repository (`seura-photos/seura-photos.github.io`, see its CLAUDE.md), marked as a draft until Nevada approves it.

The design phase (before this journal existed) is covered by devlog parts 1 to 6.
