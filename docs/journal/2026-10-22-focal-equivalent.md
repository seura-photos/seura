# The Info panel's lens, as the phone shows it (M2 slice 8)

- **Nevada compared Seura's Info panel with his iPhone** for a new HEIC photo: the phone said 24 mm ƒ/1.78; Seura said 6.8 mm ƒ/1.8.
- **Both of Seura's numbers came from the photo, but not the ones people expect.** 6.8 mm is the lens's real focal length; 24 mm is the "35 mm equivalent", how wide the shot looks next to a full-frame camera, which iPhones store separately and show instead. And Seura had rounded the f-number to one decimal, Claude's choice, where the photo records 1.78.
- **Claude suggested, and Nevada agreed:** show the 35 mm equivalent when the photo records one, with the actual focal length and the lens's name in the tooltip; show a camera's actual focal length when there's no equivalent; and show values as recorded, to two decimals (ƒ/1.78, ƒ/2.8).
