# The Folders heading showed through the Viewer

- **Found by Nevada** in build 0.1 on Windows: with a photo open in the Viewer, the sidebar's FOLDERS heading stayed on screen, over the photo.
- **Claude Code's mistake, from #52:** making the heading stay in view while the tree scrolls gave it a stacking level, so the folder rows pass under it. The Viewer, laid over the Library, had none of its own, so the heading was drawn on top of it. The Viewer now sits above everything in the Library and below menus and dialogs; checked in the real app.
